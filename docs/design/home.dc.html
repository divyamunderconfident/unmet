How it works

Every day Unmet reads new public posts that ask for something to exist, keeps the real needs, groups them into problems and ranks each by distinct people × recency × pain.

Quotes are short excerpts, always credited and linked. Stack Exchange content is CC BY-SA. Classifier precision: not measured yet.
